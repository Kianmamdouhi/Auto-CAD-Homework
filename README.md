# Industrial Drawing & Computer-Aided Design (AutoCAD)

This repository showcases a collection of industrial engineering drawings and technical drafts completed using **AutoCAD**. These projects demonstrate proficiency in geometric dimensioning and tolerancing (GD&T), orthographic projections, assembly modeling, and drafting standards.



 Skills & Competencies Demonstrated
* **2D & 3D Technical Drafting:** Accurate creation of orthographic, sectional, and auxiliary views.
* **Standards & Conventions:** Adherence to industrial drafting standards (ISO/ANSI) for line weights, dimensioning, and scaling.
* **Geometric Dimensioning & Tolerancing (GD&T):** Application of surface finishes, fits, tolerances, and geometric controls.
* **Assembly & Detail Drawings:** Creation of exploded and detailed component drawings for manufacturing.
